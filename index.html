Simple rules, real decisions

How a word ladder works

The shortest possible route is called par. You can finish with a longer ladder, but finding par is where the puzzle gets interesting.

01

Read the two words

Every puzzle starts with one four-letter word and gives you a target. In the example above, TUNE must become POOP.

02

Change one position

Replace exactly one letter. The order never changes, and skipping or adding letters is not allowed.

03

Keep every word legal

Each rung must be a recognized word in the game dictionary. Invented words and abbreviations will not move you forward.
